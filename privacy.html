Privacy policy: techdome34 uploader

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

The techdome34 uploader is a private tool used only by the techdome34 team to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel and to move our own files in our own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access: only the techdome34 team's own YouTube channel (to upload videos and read their public statistics) and the team's own Google Drive project folder. We do not access anyone else's data.

Storage and sharing: access tokens are stored only on the team's own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services: our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: message @techdome34 on Instagram.
